Meet Tsuki — the moon cub 🌙

Tsuki is the heart of Kawaii Cub Co. — a magical little brown bear cub with a golden crescent moon on his forehead, big sparkly eyes, and purple fairy wings sewn on forever. He keeps a crayon tucked on his ear, always ready for the next coloring adventure.

He stars in his very own coloring book and leads the Dreamfield Pals — seven friends who pop up throughout the whole library to cheer your little artist on.
