Privacy Policy 💜

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Kawaii Cub Co. respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Our website itself does not collect personal information directly. However:

  • Purchases: When you buy our books through Gumroad, Etsy, or other platforms, those platforms collect your name, email, and payment details to process your order. We receive your name and email so we can deliver your files.
  • Analytics: We may use basic, anonymized analytics to understand how visitors use our site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver your purchased digital products.
  • To respond to customer support requests.
  • To improve our website and products.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Children's Privacy

Our coloring books are designed for children, but our website and store are intended for adults (parents, teachers, caregivers) to make purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Cookies

Our site may use basic cookies for functionality. Third-party platforms we link to (Gumroad, Etsy, social media) have their own cookie policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Digital delivery is handled by established platforms (Gumroad, Etsy) with their own security measures.

6. Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us through our Gumroad store.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

8. Contact

Privacy questions? Reach us through our Gumroad store or the contact info on our homepage.
